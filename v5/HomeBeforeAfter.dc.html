What changed
Moved to the bell
“Your hiring” and “Your applications” cards removed from Home. New applicants, the >5 applications alert and application status changes are reached from the notification bell only (no duplicate on Home).
Removed from Home
Search bar, Skill / Role / Location filter chips and the All · Jobs · Crew · Network type chips. Filters now live on the See all screens (Discover jobs, Find talent). The mixed, interleaved feed is gone.
Reordered into fixed sections
1 Jobs for you (3 cards + subtle Premium summary) → 2 Crew to connect with / Crew for your open roles (3 cards) → 3 From your network (3 rows). Same header style and “See all” placement on every section.
Card rules
Job card: title, production house, location, duration, dates, posted time, one primary action (View & apply); Save / Share / Not interested in the ⋯ menu. Crew card: photo, name, Work Title(s), department, availability, mutual connections, one primary action (Connect); Follow / Hide in the ⋯ menu.
Moved
“Hiring for a project?” prompt now sits inside the Crew section (new users only). The Premium digest card became one subtle row at the end of Jobs for you.
