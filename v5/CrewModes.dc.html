States and motion
Default · Empty (Home with no posts, no saved jobs) · Loading skeleton (Mode selection) · Inline error (hiring setup) · Draft (hiring setup kept, Poster stays locked) · Success toasts “Job Seeker is on” / “Recruiter is on” · Coach marks once per session. Motion: overlay and sheet spring up (~0.5 s); the opt-in card collapses into the header chip with a spring; buttons ~0.18 s. With prefers-reduced-motion, springs and slides become 0.15 s fades.
