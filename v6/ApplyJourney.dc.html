Job application journey — what the applicant sees

Screen by screen, from finding a job to a final decision. Every screen below is the live prototype screen. Statuses and the 7-day categorisation window are summarised underneath. For client review and approval

Step 1
Find a job
Search and filter. Each card shows the job dates, location and fee (or “Fee not disclosed”).
Step 2
Read the job details
Name of the Production House, Department, job dates, fee and the recruiter’s full requirements.
Step 3
Confirm and send
Tap Apply now. A sheet shows exactly what the recruiter will see; the applicant confirms and sends.

Step 4
Sent — waiting to be categorised
Confirmation shown; the application reads “Uncategorised” until the recruiter reviews it (within 7 days).
Step 5
Track every application
Jobs I Have Applied To — with job dates, status and a note when an application lapses after 7 days.
Step 6
Decision and remarks
The applicant is notified of each status change. The recruiter’s remarks are visible to the applicant.
Application statuses (applicant view)
Uncategorised
Sent. The recruiter hasn’t categorised it yet. Recruiters must categorise within 7 days.
Under Review
The recruiter is actively reviewing the application.
Shortlisted
Moved to the shortlist, with the recruiter’s remarks.
On Hold
Kept for later; the requirement may still be open.
SelectedRejected
Final decision; the requirement can then be closed.
No longer under review
Not categorised within 7 days. The applicant is notified and can apply elsewhere.
Every status change sends a notification. Messaging is not part of this phase.
