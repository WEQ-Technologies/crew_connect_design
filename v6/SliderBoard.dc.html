Decisions, states and motion
Chosen direction A, Glass wash: the tint lives in a soft wash and a 12% oversized icon, so each card is distinct, the row reads as one set, and labels sit on near-white for full contrast. B is louder than five cards in a row need; C’s glow fades into the page gradient. Page dots under the row: with 3–5 cards they show how many there are and where you are, which a thin progress bar can’t; they track the snap position. Your jobs keeps one height (the taller tab); an empty tab centres its message inside it and keeps both buttons, so the feed never jumps. Default tab: last viewed; first time, the tab with new activity, else Applications [TBC]. States: default, empty, loading skeleton, “Job Seeker/Recruiter is on” toast from the opt-in. Motion: cards slide in staggered from the right and press to 98%; tabs cross-fade with a 10 px slide; reduced motion uses 0.15 s fades only.
