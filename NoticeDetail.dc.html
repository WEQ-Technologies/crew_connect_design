Revised minimum day rates for camera and lighting crew — effective 1 December

Published 27 Oct 2026Edited 27 Oct, 4:10 pm

Following the general body meeting held on 20 October 2026, the Guild has approved revised minimum day rates for camera and lighting crew working on feature films, web series and advertising projects in Maharashtra.

The revised rates apply to all engagements that begin on or after 1 December 2026. Engagements already signed before this date will continue at the agreed rates.

Role (12-hour shift)Minimum / day
Focus Puller₹8,500
2nd Assistant Camera₹5,000
Lighting Technician₹3,800

Members with questions can contact the Guild office between 10 am and 6 pm, Monday to Saturday.
